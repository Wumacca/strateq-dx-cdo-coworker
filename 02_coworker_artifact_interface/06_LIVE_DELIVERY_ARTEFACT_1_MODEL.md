# Live Delivery Artefact 1 Model

## Status

Artefact 1 Rev1 is the canonical live delivery meeting and reporting record. Preserve its approved agenda and layout. Do not redesign it through this method file.

## Purpose

Provide one controlled delivery-cycle record that reconciles delivery evidence, PEP position, controls, decisions and upward reporting.

## Required information basis

Artefact 1 is prepared from:

- the approved `Initiative Delivery Setup`;
- the current PEP/client-control plan;
- the Initiative Evidence and Decision File;
- supplier/developer progress evidence;
- current RAID, decisions, changes and acceptance/readiness evidence;
- Digital Lead confirmations made in the session.

It is a reporting record, not another live delivery plan.

## Controlled narrative

Where present in the approved template, these fields must be evidence-led:

- Current Position
- Achievements / movement since last review
- Next Control Move
- 7 Day Lookahead
- Decisions or escalations required
- Delivery health and go-live confidence

The Coworker may draft these from governed records and must identify the source. It must not infer a positive or negative narrative from an unsupported percentage or vendor claim.

## Vendor-led delivery

Record vendor progress against agreed client milestones and evidence criteria. Do not reproduce the vendor's full internal plan. Unsupported or conflicting claims are marked `Pending confirmation` and escalated for resolution.

## Financial boundary

Show financial information only to the extent allowed by the `Digital financial-reporting basis` in `Initiative Delivery Setup`. Where another client role owns the budget, state that owner and restrict Digital's narrative to the agreed summary, variations or emerging impacts.

## Mobilisation handover reference

The first Artefact 1 record after approval to enter delivery includes the approved `Initiative Delivery Setup` reference once. Later Artefact 1 records do not repeat the handover unless the setup baseline is formally revised.

## Approval boundary

The Coworker prepares the record. Meeting owners confirm content, and the Digital Lead approves governed changes or escalation. Artefact 1 cannot approve scope, cost, acceptance, go-live or lifecycle transitions.
